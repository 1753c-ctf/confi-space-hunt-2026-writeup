# 17 53C CTF - Space Flight Crew Flags

## T-shirt - Space Flight Crew

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_who_hack}`

**Solution:**  
The task required a bit of real-world perceptiveness. The flag was printed directly on the t-shirts of the space flight crew, the organizers of the 17 53C CTF.

## Main Stage - Pre-flight Briefing

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_on_st@g3}`

**Solution:**  
The flag was presented on the main stage during the pre-flight briefing, during the cosmic lunch break. For participants who stayed upstairs, it was also made available digitally on the 1753c Discord server.

## Registration - Spaceship on the Launchpad

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_m1llenn1um_f@lc0n}`

**Solution:**  
The flag was located at the registration desk, the place where the entire mission took off. Its content referred to the Millennium Falcon.

## Cosmonaut Balloon - Farewell to the Cosmonauts

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_event_h@riz0n}`

**Solution:**  
The clue pointed to the place where cosmonauts bid farewell to the world before boarding the rocket. The flag was physically located near the stairs by the entrance.

## Scoreboard - Launch Screen

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_ent3rpr15e_b0ldly_g0}`

**Solution:**  
The rocket's launch screen was the event scoreboard. The flag was placed below the results displayed on one of the TVs in the conference area.

## ING Ice Cream

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_th4t_t4st3s_g00d}`

**Solution:**  
One of the tastiest flags at the event. It was enough to locate the ING ice cream stand / machine at the venue.

## Presentation - Space Consulting I

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_fr00m_s113m}`

**Solution:**  
The flag was hidden in the materials during Daniel Jeczen's talk:  
**"[PL] SIEM without secrets: Building an efficient platform on a modern Open Source stack"**.

## Presentation - Space Consulting II

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_about_0000}`

**Solution:**  
The second space consulting flag appeared during the talk by Krzysztof Kuznik and Marcin Marynczak about transitioning from fragmented security to scalable models. The flag content, `about_0000`, referenced Zero Trust architecture.

## Spaceship Landing in the Parking Lot

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_0n_th3_br1ck}`

**Solution:**  
A classic orienteering task. Participants had to go outside to the parking lot in front of the building, the landing pad, and look down. The flag was written in chalk directly on the paving stones.

## WIFI Flag - Spaceship's Connection to Earth

**Flag:** `confi{the_one_N0t_0n_Th3_w1R3}`

**Solution:**  
The "connection to Earth" was the event's wireless network. Participants received an encrypted Base58 string:

```text
LvbqEwFQUnhi5TmhndBv6uYGuKrzh8r4r386qkjuv
```

After decoding it, for example with CyberChef using the `From Base58` recipe, the correct flag text was revealed.
